BTEC Higher National comes in two stages: HNC at Level 4 and HND at Level 5, with HND building directly on top of HNC as its first year. Neither is a full honours degree on its own, but both are recognised, stand-alone qualifications that commonly serve as a stepping stone into one. This page explains what each level actually involves and how the progression to a full degree typically works.

What Is a BTEC HNC?

BTEC HNC, the Higher National Certificate, sits at Level 4 and carries 120 credits, roughly one year of full-time study. It's academically equivalent to the first year of a bachelor's degree, and all assessment at this level is internal, with no Pearson-set external exams.

What Is a BTEC HND?

BTEC HND, the Higher National Diploma, sits at Level 5 and carries 240 credits in total, the HNC's 120 credits plus a further 120. It typically takes around two years of full-time study and is equivalent to the second year of a bachelor's degree.

Is HNC or HND a Degree?

Neither is a full honours degree by itself. Both are recognised, stand-alone qualifications at their respective RQF levels, and both commonly serve as a route into one, rather than a substitute for a completed degree.

How HND Leads to a Full University Degree

Most HND graduates complete one further year at a university, often called a "top-up" year, to convert their qualification into a full BA or BSc honours degree. Entry to a specific top-up programme depends on the receiving university's own requirements, so it's worth checking those early rather than assuming automatic acceptance.

Can I go straight to HND without doing HNC first?

Most colleges require HNC as HND's first stage, though some centres offer direct HND entry for students with sufficient prior qualifications. Check with your specific centre.

Is HND equivalent to a full degree for job applications?

HND is equivalent to the second year of a bachelor's degree, not a full honours degree. Most employers and universities recognise it as such, distinct from a completed degree.

How long does it take to convert an HND to a full degree?

Typically one further year of full-time study, known as a top-up year, at a university, though this varies by institution and subject.

Do universities accept BTEC HND for degree top-up programmes?

Most UK universities offering top-up degrees accept BTEC HND, though specific grade and subject requirements vary from course to course.
