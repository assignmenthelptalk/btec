BTEC Higher Nationals come in two stages: the HNC at Level 4 and the HND at Level 5. This page explains what each involves, how they are assessed and graded, and what Pearson says about progression to a degree. It follows Pearson's Higher Nationals in Business specification (Issue 3, January 2022). Other Higher National subjects have their own specifications, so check yours with your centre.

What Is a BTEC HNC?

The HNC is a Level 4 qualification of 120 credits: eight 15-credit units, six core and two optional, in the Business HNC. Pearson says it is usually studied full time over one year or part time over two. It describes the HNC as a solid grounding in the subject that students can build on if they continue.

What Is a BTEC HND?

The HND is a Level 5 qualification of 240 credits: 120 at Level 4 (eight units) and 120 at Level 5 (seven units). Pearson says it is usually studied full time over two years or part time over four. The Level 5 units let students specialise, and the overall HND grade is based on the Level 5 units only.

Pearson describes its Higher Nationals as set out in line with the Quality Assurance Agency's Framework for Higher Education qualification standards at Levels 4 and 5. Pearson also lists Higher Nationals at Levels 6 and 7 in its wider range, so the HND is not the top of the suite.

How Higher Nationals Are Assessed

Pearson describes internal assignments that centres set and assess, and Pearson-set assignments that centres set in line with Pearson's guidelines and grade. The Business specification does not describe Pearson-set exams. Pearson sets an annual theme for its Pearson-set units, and the centre writes the assignment.

Pearson says a student whose assignment does not pass must have the opportunity to resubmit it once, capped at a Pass, and a student who still fails the unit may be allowed to repeat it once, again capped at a Pass. See our HNC and HND pages for the details.

Is an HNC or HND a Degree?

Neither is a full honours degree by itself. Pearson says the Level 5 HND is recognised by higher education providers as meeting admission requirements to many relevant courses, and gives examples such as BA (Hons) Business Management, and that many universities allow advanced entry to the second or third year through credit transfer, articulation agreements or case-by-case admission. Pearson also says each university sets its own admission criteria, which can change, and points students to the degree finder on highernationals.com. Check the course page of any university you are considering and ask about the exact entry year.

Studying an HNC or HND right now? The unit criteria in your Assignment Brief, and the referencing and writing standard your centre sets, are what decide the grade. Read both before you start.

Referencing at Higher National Level

Pearson's specification does not set a referencing style. It does say centres should give students a guide on how to use and reference source materials and avoid plagiarism, and it lists active research and effective writing among the study skills the qualification develops. Your centre or Assignment Brief names the style, which is often Harvard. See referencing for BTEC HNC and HND.

Can I go straight to HND without doing HNC first?

The HND includes 120 credits at Level 4 as well as 120 at Level 5, so you either study the Level 4 units or have that level recognised. Whether you can start directly depends on your centre's admission and recognition-of-prior-learning policy, so ask your centre.

Is an HND equivalent to a full degree?

No. The HND is a Level 5 qualification, and a bachelor's degree with honours is Level 6. Pearson says the HND is recognised as meeting admission requirements to many relevant degree courses, and that many universities allow advanced entry, but each university sets its own criteria.

How long does it take to go from an HND to a degree?

It depends on the university and the course. Pearson says many universities allow advanced entry to the second or third year and that agreements include credit transfer, articulation and case-by-case admission. Check the course page for the entry year and the length of the remaining study.

Do universities accept BTEC HND?

Pearson says higher education providers recognise the HND as meeting admission requirements to many relevant courses, but each university sets its own admission criteria and they can change. Use the university's course page and Pearson's degree finder on highernationals.com for current requirements.
