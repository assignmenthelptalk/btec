This page covers Pearson's BTEC Level 3 National in Business (2016) and, briefly, the Higher National Certificate and Diploma in Business at Levels 4 and 5. It sets out the sizes, which units are Pearson-set and which your centre marks, and how to approach each kind. The National section follows Pearson's specification (Issue 14, April 2023) and the Higher National section follows its Higher Nationals in Business specification (Issue 3, January 2022). The two have different units, so check your registration first. Pearson also lists a 2025 range of qualifications with their own units.

BTEC National Business: Sizes and Structure

The specification covers five sizes. Pearson says the Extended Diploma is a two-year, full-time course that meets entry requirements in its own right for higher education courses in business areas, and that it can also support progression to employment in business roles and to Higher Apprenticeships.

The Mandatory Units in the Diploma

The Diploma has six mandatory units, three external and three internal, plus two optional units. Learners must achieve at least a Near Pass in each mandatory external unit and a Pass or above in each mandatory internal unit. The Extended Diploma has its own unit list, so check the structure for your own size in the specification.

The Three External Units

  • Unit 2: Developing a Marketing Campaign. A task set and marked by Pearson under supervised conditions, completed on a computer and worth 70 marks. The Part A research pack is released in the afternoon of the day before Part B, and learners have a two-hour preparation period with it. Part B is a three-hour supervised session the next morning. The content covers the principles and purposes of marketing, using information to develop the rationale for a campaign, and planning and developing a campaign. It is available in December/January and May/June.
  • Unit 3: Personal and Business Finance. A written examination set and marked by Pearson, lasting two hours and worth 80 marks. Section A covers personal finance and carries roughly one third of the marks, and Section B covers business finance and carries roughly two thirds. The content includes managing personal finance, the purpose of accounting, sources of business finance, and break-even and cash flow forecasts. It is available in January and May/June.
  • Unit 6: Principles of Management. A task set and marked by Pearson under supervised conditions, worth 88 marks. Part A is released one week before Part B, which is a single three-hour supervised session timetabled by Pearson. The unit looks at how management and leadership in the workplace contribute to business success. It is available in December/January and May/June.

Pearson says learners may resit external assessments during their programme, and its current retake policy applies, so ask your centre.

The Internal Units

For internal units, read the learning aims in the specification and match each task in your Assignment Brief to the criteria it targets.

  • Unit 1: Exploring Business. The learning aims are the features of different businesses and what makes them successful, how businesses are organised, the environment in which businesses operate, and business markets.
  • Unit 4: Managing an Event. Learners explore the role of an event organiser, investigate the feasibility of a proposed event, develop a detailed plan for a business or social enterprise event, stage and manage it, and reflect on it and on their own skills development.
  • Unit 5: International Business. The learning aims are the international context for business operations, the international economic environment, the external factors that influence international businesses, and cultural factors.

Internal units are graded Pass, Merit or Distinction. For how those criteria work, see BTEC grading criteria explained.

BTEC Higher National Business at Levels 4 and 5

In Pearson's Higher Nationals in Business, the HNC is a Level 4 qualification of 120 credits, made up of eight 15-credit units: six core units and two optional. Pearson says it is usually studied full time over one year or part time over two. The HND is 240 credits, with 120 at Level 4 (eight units) and 120 at Level 5 (seven units), usually studied full time over two years.

The HNC core units are Business and the Business Environment, Marketing Processes and Planning, Human Resource Management, Leadership and Management, Accounting Principles, and Managing a Successful Business Project, which is Pearson-set. HND learners also take a Level 5 Pearson-set unit, the Research Project, with other Level 5 units such as Organisational Behaviour.

Pearson describes the assessment as internal assignments that centres set and Pearson-set assignments that centres set in line with Pearson's guidelines, and your centre grades both. The specification does not describe Pearson-set exams. Units are graded Pass, Merit or Distinction. If a student's assignment does not pass, Pearson says they must have the opportunity to resubmit it, once only, and the grade for a reassessed assignment is capped at a Pass. A student who still fails the unit may be allowed to repeat it once, again capped at a Pass.

What separates Merit from Distinction in a Business assignment? The answer is in the criteria your brief targets. Read each command verb, such as explain, analyse or evaluate, and check that every part of your answer, including any case study or business data, does what that verb asks for.

Progression and Registration Dates

Pearson says the Foundation Diploma supports progression to an Apprenticeship in the business sector or to a further year of study at Level 3, and that the smaller sizes support progression to higher education when taken as part of a programme that includes other BTEC Nationals or A levels. BTEC Nationals attract UCAS points, and the amount depends on your grade and qualification size.

Pearson's qualification page lists last-registration dates that differ by size, funding route and nation. When we checked in November 2026, DfE-funded registrations in England showed 31 July 2028 for the Certificate to the Diploma and 31 July 2027 for the Extended Diploma, and other registrations in England, Northern Ireland and Wales showed 31 July 2030. Check with your centre which qualification you are registered on.

Which BTEC National Business units are externally assessed?

In Pearson's 2016 National, Unit 2 Developing a Marketing Campaign (a Pearson-set task, 70 marks), Unit 3 Personal and Business Finance (a two-hour exam, 80 marks) and Unit 6 Principles of Management (a Pearson-set task, 88 marks) are external, and the Extended Diploma has a fourth external unit. Every size includes at least one. Units such as Exploring Business are marked by your centre.

Is there a Business Environment unit in BTEC Business?

Business and the Business Environment is Unit 1 of Pearson's Higher National in Business (Level 4, 15 credits), and it is assessed through centre-marked assignments. It is not a unit of the 2016 National, where Unit 1 is Exploring Business and is internally assessed.

How long is the Managing a Successful Business Project at BTEC Business?

Pearson's specification does not set a word count. In the Higher National, Managing a Successful Business Project is a Pearson-set core unit at Level 4, studied by both HNC and HND learners, and the HND also has a Level 5 Pearson-set Research Project. Your centre sets the length in the Assignment Brief, so follow the brief.

Do I need Harvard referencing for BTEC National Business assignments?

Pearson's specification does not set a referencing system for the National or the Higher National. Your centre or Assignment Brief decides, so follow the style it names and cite your sources consistently.
