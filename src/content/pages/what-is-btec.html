BTEC is a family of applied, vocational qualifications awarded by Pearson and regulated on the Ofqual Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF). The name originated with the Business and Technology Education Council, and the BTEC brand and assessment model now sit with Pearson, the same organisation behind Edexcel. Pearson's range runs from introductory levels up to Higher Nationals at Levels 4 to 7. This page covers the main routes most students meet: BTEC First, National, HNC and HND.

What Does BTEC Stand For and Who Awards It?

BTEC originally stood for the Business and Technology Education Council, a body that developed vocational qualifications in the 1980s. Pearson later acquired the brand and now awards current BTEC qualifications. The correct current terminology is "Pearson BTEC," though most students and colleges simply say "BTEC."

Every BTEC qualification sits on the Ofqual-regulated RQF, the same framework A levels and university degrees use to define level. That is what lets universities, employers and other awarding bodies compare a BTEC with other UK qualifications.

BTEC National sits at Level 3. In Pearson's 2016 specifications it comes in five sizes: the Certificate (0.5 of an A level), Extended Certificate (one), Foundation Diploma (1.5), Diploma (two) and Extended Diploma (three). Pearson also lists a 2025 range of Nationals. See BTEC National assignment help.

BTEC HNC and HND are Level 4 and Level 5 Higher Nationals. Pearson says the HNC is usually studied over one year full time or two part time, and the HND over two years full time or four part time. See BTEC HNC and HND explained.

How Is BTEC Assessed?

A-levels are mainly assessed by exams at the end of a course. BTEC units are different: many are assessed through assignment briefs issued unit by unit, each setting out a task, the evidence required and the Pass, Merit and Distinction criteria to meet.

For internally assessed units, your tutor marks the work, a different member of staff at your centre internally verifies a sample, and Pearson's standards verifiers later sample the centre's work. Other units are set and marked by Pearson. In the 2016 Nationals every size includes at least one external unit, and the share varies by subject and size. Higher Nationals use internal and Pearson-set assignments that your centre grades. See how BTEC internal assessment works and BTEC external assessment explained.

How Is BTEC Graded?

On internally assessed units, your grade depends on which criteria you meet, labelled P1, P2, M1, D1 and so on in the brief. Pearson's rule is that a Merit needs every Merit criterion and every Pass criterion, and a Distinction needs all three bands. Externally assessed National units are marked by Pearson.

Distinction* is a qualification grade for BTEC Nationals, calculated from the unit results, and it is never awarded on a single unit. For the full breakdown, see the BTEC grading system and BTEC grading criteria explained.

Which BTEC level and subject applies to you? A BTEC National student and an HND student work to different specifications and assessment rules, even though both are called BTEC. The right next step depends on your level and subject.

How BTEC Compares to A-Levels

A-levels are assessed mainly through exams at the end of the course. A BTEC National mixes centre-marked units and Pearson-set assessment, so the balance depends on the subject and size. Both generate UCAS points, and a BTEC National Extended Diploma at D*D*D* is worth the same 168 points as three A* grades. See BTEC vs A-level for the full comparison.

Is BTEC equivalent to A-levels?

A BTEC National is a Level 3 qualification, the same level as A levels, and the two attract UCAS points on the same tariff. A BTEC National Extended Diploma graded D*D*D* carries the same 168 points as three A* grades. The qualifications are assessed differently, and universities set their own entry requirements.

Is BTEC a degree?

No. BTEC Nationals are Level 3, and the HNC and HND are Levels 4 and 5. A bachelor's degree with honours is Level 6. Pearson says the HND is recognised as meeting admission requirements to many relevant degree courses, and that many universities allow advanced entry, but each university sets its own criteria.

Who marks BTEC assignments?

For internal units, your own tutor marks the work, a different member of staff at your centre internally verifies a sample, and Pearson's standards verifiers sample the centre's work. Other units are set and marked directly by Pearson, as with the external units in the Nationals.

Can adults study BTEC HNC or HND?

Yes. Higher Nationals are studied full time or part time, and Pearson says the HNC is usually one year full time or two part time. Ask your centre about its entry requirements and how its part-time routes work.
