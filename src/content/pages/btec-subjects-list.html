Pearson says its BTEC Nationals cover 14 subject areas, and its own listing groups the qualifications under headings from Business to Travel and Tourism. This page sets out the subjects Pearson lists, shows how they are grouped across the BTEC Nationals, the newer 2025 qualifications and the Higher Nationals, and links to our subject guides. Lists change as Pearson updates its range, so confirm your own qualification with your centre.

BTEC Nationals from 2025

Pearson's 2025 brochure lists eight Alternative Academic Qualifications, each at 360 guided learning hours, first taught in September 2025: Applied Science, Computing, Construction, Early Childhood Development, Engineering, Health and Social Care, Information Technology, and Medical Science. These sit alongside other post-16 qualifications, and Pearson's Construction specification describes its version as equivalent to one A level and designed to be studied as part of a programme that includes A levels. Their assessment rules differ from the earlier Nationals.

BTEC Higher Nationals

Pearson describes the Higher Nationals as operating at Levels 4 and 5 (the HNC and the HND), equivalent to the first and second years of a university degree, with progression to both university and employment. Pearson's pages show subject areas that include Business, Computing, Construction and the Built Environment, Health and Social Care, Engineering, and Social and Community Work, and its range changes, so confirm what your college offers.

How to Check Your Own Qualification

Your qualification's exact title, size and number appear on your registration confirmation, and Pearson's specification for it lists the units. Qualification size changes the unit count and the share of external assessment, even within one subject.

Sources: Pearson's BTEC Nationals 2016 to 2020 and BTEC Nationals pages, its BTEC Nationals 2025 brochure and its BTEC Higher Nationals page.

What is the most popular BTEC subject?

It depends on the qualification size and the year. Pearson publishes results reports that show entries by subject, so use the latest one for current figures rather than a general ranking.

Can I study BTEC in more than one subject?

Often yes. Pearson designs some BTEC Nationals, such as the Extended Certificate, which is equivalent in size to one A level, to be taken alongside A levels or other BTEC Nationals. Which combinations are allowed is decided by your college.

Which BTEC subjects are available at HNC and HND level?

Pearson's Higher Nationals pages show subject areas that include Business, Computing, Construction and the Built Environment, Health and Social Care, Engineering, and Social and Community Work. Availability varies from one college to another.

Is BTEC Science a real qualification?

Yes. Pearson lists Applied Science (2016) among its BTEC Nationals, and its 2025 range includes Applied Science and Medical Science. Check which one your college offers.
