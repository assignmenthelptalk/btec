Pearson says its BTEC Nationals cover 14 subject areas, and its own listing groups the qualifications under headings from Business to Travel and Tourism. This page sets out the subjects Pearson lists, shows how they are grouped across the BTEC Nationals, the newer 2025 qualifications and the Higher Nationals, and links to our subject guides. Lists change as Pearson updates its range, so confirm your own qualification with your centre.

BTEC Nationals from 2025

Pearson's 2025 brochure lists eight Alternative Academic Qualifications, each at 360 guided learning hours, first taught in September 2025: Applied Science, Computing, Construction, Early Childhood Development, Engineering, Health and Social Care, Information Technology, and Medical Science. These sit alongside other post-16 qualifications, and Pearson's Construction specification describes its version as equivalent to one A level and designed to be studied as part of a programme that includes A levels. Their assessment rules differ from the earlier Nationals; see BTEC external assessment explained.

BTEC Higher Nationals

Pearson describes the Higher Nationals as operating at Levels 4 and 5 (the HNC and the HND), equivalent to the first and second years of a university degree, with progression to both university and employment. Pearson's pages show subject areas that include Business, Computing, Construction and the Built Environment, Health and Social Care, Engineering, and Social and Community Work, and its range changes, so confirm what your college offers. See BTEC Higher Nationals explained.

How to Check Your Own Qualification

Your qualification's exact title, size and number appear on your registration confirmation, and Pearson's specification for it lists the units. Qualification size changes the unit count and the share of external assessment, even within one subject. See BTEC Level 2 explained for the qualifications below Level 3, and BTEC assignment help for an overview.

What is the most popular BTEC subject?

Business and Health and Social Care are consistently the highest-enrolment BTEC National subjects in the UK, followed closely by Engineering and IT.

Can I study BTEC in more than one subject?

Combining two full BTEC National qualifications is uncommon, but some colleges allow a BTEC alongside A-level subjects, or a smaller BTEC Award or Certificate in a second area.

Which BTEC subjects are available at HNC and HND level?

Business, Health and Social Care, Engineering, and IT are the subjects most commonly offered at Higher National level, though availability varies from one college to another.

Is BTEC Science a real qualification?

Yes. BTEC Applied Science exists as a National-level qualification, though it's offered by fewer centres than subjects like Business or Health and Social Care.
