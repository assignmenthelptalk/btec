BTEC covers eleven primary applied and vocational subject areas, from Business and Health and Social Care through to Engineering, IT, and beyond. This page indexes every subject BTEC offers, what each one typically covers, and which levels it's available at, so you can find the right subject-specific guidance quickly.

BTEC Business

Covers business environment, marketing, finance, human resources, and entrepreneurship units. Available at National, HNC, and HND. See BTEC Business assignment help.

BTEC Health and Social Care

Covers human lifespan development, safeguarding, care values, and communication in care settings. Available at National and HND. See BTEC Health and Social Care assignment help.

BTEC Engineering

Covers engineering principles, mathematics, mechanical and electrical science, and project design. Available at National, HNC, and HND. See BTEC Engineering assignment help.

BTEC IT

Covers networking, programming, database design, cybersecurity, and systems analysis. Available at National, HNC, and HND. See BTEC IT assignment help.

BTEC Sport

Covers anatomy and physiology, fitness testing, coaching, and sports psychology. Available primarily at National. See BTEC Sport assignment help.

BTEC Public Services

Covers government, citizenship, teamwork, physical fitness, and crime and its effects. Available at National. See BTEC Public Services assignment help.

BTEC Childcare and Early Years

Covers child development theories, safeguarding, play and learning, and legislation. Available at National. See BTEC Childcare and Early Years assignment help.

What is the most popular BTEC subject?

Business and Health and Social Care are consistently the highest-enrolment BTEC National subjects in the UK, followed closely by Engineering and IT.

Can I study BTEC in more than one subject?

Combining two full BTEC National qualifications is uncommon, but some colleges allow a BTEC alongside A-level subjects, or a smaller BTEC Award or Certificate in a second area.

Which BTEC subjects are available at HNC and HND level?

Business, Health and Social Care, Engineering, and IT are the subjects most commonly offered at Higher National level, though availability varies from one college to another.

Is BTEC Science a real qualification?

Yes. BTEC Applied Science exists as a National-level qualification, though it's offered by fewer centres than subjects like Business or Health and Social Care.
