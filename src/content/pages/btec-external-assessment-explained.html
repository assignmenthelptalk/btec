Most BTEC units are marked by your own tutor, but a specific subset, particularly at National level, are set and marked directly by Pearson instead. This is called external assessment, and it comes with a completely different format and a much stricter resubmission rule than internal coursework. Here's how to identify which of your units fall into this category and how to prepare for them.

What Is BTEC External Assessment?

Pearson writes and marks the task directly, rather than the student's own tutor. Depending on the unit, this takes the form of a traditional written exam or a Pearson-set assignment completed under controlled conditions over a defined period.

How to Identify Which of Your Units Are Externally Assessed

Check the unit specification on Pearson's BTEC qualification page, or look at your centre's assessment timetable. Externally assessed units appear on a fixed, scheduled sitting date, unlike ongoing coursework deadlines set by your tutor.

Format Differences: Timed Exam vs Pearson-Set Assignment

Some external units are traditional written exams sat under exam-hall conditions. Others are Pearson-set assignment tasks completed over a defined controlled period, closer in structure to coursework but marked externally. Business Environment, a unit within BTEC National Business, is a commonly cited example of an externally assessed unit.

No Resubmission on External Assessment: What This Means

Unlike internal units, a referred or failed externally assessed unit cannot be resubmitted. The student must resit in a future Pearson assessment window instead, typically offered once or twice a year, which makes preparation before the original sitting considerably more important than for internal coursework.

How do you prepare differently for an external unit? The no-resubmission stakes mean exam-specific preparation matters more here than for any internally assessed unit.

Preparing for a Pearson-Set External Assessment

Command-word analysis, timed response planning, and structured paragraph writing under exam conditions are the core skills that separate strong external-assessment performance from strong coursework performance. Are all BTEC units externally assessed?

No. The majority of BTEC units are internally assessed by your tutor. Only a specific subset per subject and level carries external Pearson-set assessment.

Can I resit a failed BTEC external exam?

Yes, but not as a resubmission. You must resit the exam or task in a future scheduled Pearson assessment window, typically offered once or twice a year.

How do I know if my Business Environment unit is externally assessed?

Business Environment is a commonly externally assessed unit within BTEC National Business. Confirm the exact assessment method on your unit specification or with your tutor, since specifications can be updated.

Is external assessment harder than internal assessment?

It isn't inherently harder, but it demands different skills: timed exam technique and command-word precision, rather than sustained coursework development over several weeks.
