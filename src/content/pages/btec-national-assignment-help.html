BTEC National is Pearson's Level 3 vocational qualification, available in several sizes from the Certificate to the Extended Diploma. Some units are marked by your centre against criteria, and others are set and marked by Pearson. This page sets out how the sizes work, who marks what, how the grade is built and what the UCAS tariff gives, so you can approach each kind of unit correctly. It follows Pearson's 2016 National specifications (for example Business, Issue 14, April 2023) and its Centre Guide to internal assessment (version 4.1). Pearson also lists a 2025 range of Nationals with different rules, so confirm which one you are registered on.

What BTEC National Is and How It Differs from A-Levels

BTEC Nationals are Level 3 qualifications. Pearson describes the Extended Diploma in subjects such as Business as a two-year, full-time course that meets entry requirements in its own right for related higher education courses, and says the smaller sizes support progression when taken with other BTEC Nationals or A levels.

A-levels are mainly assessed by exams. Both qualifications attract UCAS points and are accepted by universities, though each university sets its own entry requirements.

Internal Assessment at BTEC National

For internally assessed units, your centre sets the assignment brief (or uses a Pearson authorised brief), and your tutor judges your evidence against the Pass, Merit and Distinction criteria. Pearson's rule for the unit grade is the highest level for which you have met all the criteria, which means a Distinction needs every Distinction, Merit and Pass criterion. Work that does not satisfy the Pass criteria is reported as Unclassified.

A different member of staff at your centre internally verifies a sample of the marking, and Pearson's standards verifiers later sample the centre's work.

Pearson allows one resubmission per assignment, authorised by your Lead Internal Verifier, with a deadline within 15 working days of your results and not capped at a Pass. After that, one retake may be authorised in exceptional circumstances.

External Assessment at BTEC National

External units are set and marked by Pearson and taken under specified conditions. Pearson's specifications describe exams, where all learners take the same assessment at the same time, and set tasks completed in a defined window. Some tasks include a preparation period with set information.

  • Business Unit 3, Personal and Business Finance: a two-hour written exam worth 80 marks.
  • Business Unit 2, Developing a Marketing Campaign: a supervised task worth 70 marks.
  • Sport Unit 1, Anatomy and Physiology: a written exam lasting one hour 30 minutes, worth 80 marks.

Pearson says learners may resit external assessments during their programme and refers centres to its current retake policy, and it says that where there is more than one attempt the best result counts, up to the permitted maximum. Resits are not resubmissions, so ask your centre how many attempts you have and when the sittings are.

How the BTEC National Grade Is Built

Internal units are graded Distinction, Merit, Pass or Unclassified. External units add Near Pass, which gives credit below a Pass for positive performance. To be awarded a qualification, a learner must complete all units, achieve at least a Near Pass in every external unit and a Pass or above in the mandatory units, and reach the minimum points for a qualification grade.

The overall grade is an aggregate. Every unit contributes in proportion to its guided learning hours, so a 120-hour unit counts double a 60-hour unit. Pearson says there is some element of compensation, meaning a higher performance in some units can balance a lower one in others. Distinction* is awarded for the qualification as a whole and not for a single unit. A learner who does not reach the minimum is recorded as Unclassified and is not certificated.

The criteria themselves still work unit by unit. Each starts with a command verb that sets the type of response, so match each part of your answer to the verb for the criterion it targets. See BTEC grading criteria explained.

BTEC National UCAS Points and University Entry

BTEC Nationals attract UCAS points on the same scale as A levels. The points are awarded for the whole qualification, so the total depends on the size and the overall grade profile, such as DDM. Use the BTEC UCAS points calculator to convert your grades.

What does a Distinction-level answer look like in a BTEC National assignment? It does what the command verb asks for, on every criterion your brief targets, and every part of the answer can be traced to a criterion.

Writing to the Criteria

Read each criterion and note its verb and its topic. If the verb asks you to evaluate, weigh the evidence and reach a conclusion that the evidence supports, instead of listing points. Name the criterion your section addresses, so your tutor can see which evidence meets which criterion. A criterion that is implied but never directly addressed is hard to award.

Use more than one source or example where the brief asks you to consider more than one perspective, and use the referencing style your centre or brief names.

Progressing from BTEC National to Higher Nationals

Pearson says its Higher Nationals are designed for students who want a clear line of sight to employment and to a degree at Level 6, and it describes the Level 4 HNC as a grounding in the subject that students can build on. The Higher Nationals use different units from the Nationals and are assessed through centre-graded assignments. For the full qualification overview, see BTEC assignment help.

Does BTEC National assignment help cover both internal and external assessment units?

Yes, but they need different approaches. For internal units we map your evidence to the Pass, Merit and Distinction criteria in your brief. For external units, which Pearson sets and marks, we focus on command-word analysis, timed responses and practising the question style, because there is no coursework draft to improve.

What UCAS points does a BTEC National Extended Diploma give you?

A D*D*D* Extended Diploma gives 168 UCAS points, the same as A*A*A* at A level. The points are awarded for the overall qualification grade, not per unit. The table above gives every Extended Diploma grade combination. Check each university's offer conditions, because entry requirements vary.

What is the difference between BTEC National Pass and Distinction criteria?

Each criterion has a command verb that sets the type of response. Pearson's rule is that a Distinction needs every Distinction criterion and every Pass and Merit criterion to be satisfied, and a Pass is a defined level of performance that cannot be given just for completing the assignment. The exact wording differs for every unit, so use the criteria in your own brief.

Can I get BTEC National assignment help if I did not pass a unit?

Yes. Pearson's rules allow one resubmission per assignment if your Lead Internal Verifier authorises it, with a deadline within 15 working days of your results, and one retake in exceptional circumstances. We identify which criteria were not met and structure a response for them. The resubmission is not capped at a Pass under the Centre Guide, but the conditions apply, so check with your centre.
