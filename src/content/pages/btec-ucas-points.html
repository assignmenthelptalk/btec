Under the current UCAS Tariff, a BTEC National Extended Diploma graded D*D*D* is worth 168 points, the same as three A* grades at A-level. This page sets out the full BTEC National tariff table across all three qualification sizes, so you can convert your actual or predicted grades into a points figure and check it against a university offer.

BTEC National UCAS Tariff Table: Every Grade Combination

Extended Diploma (three A-level equivalent): D*D*D* = 168, D*D*D = 160, DDD = 144, DDM = 128, DMM = 112, MMM = 96, MMP = 80, MPP = 64, PPP = 48.

Diploma (2.5 A-level equivalent): D*D* = 112, D*D = 104, DD = 96, DM = 80, MM = 64, MP = 48, PP = 32.

Extended Certificate (one A-level equivalent): D* = 56, D = 48, M = 32, P = 16.

These figures come from the 2017 UCAS Tariff scale, the system currently used across the sector for comparing qualifications of different types.

How to Read a University Offer Against Your BTEC Grades

Universities usually state offers as a total points figure, such as "112 points," rather than naming a specific BTEC grade combination. To check whether your predicted grades meet an offer, find your qualification size in the table above, locate your predicted combination, and compare its points total directly against the number in the offer.

A DDM Extended Diploma prediction, for example, meets a 128-point offer exactly. A DMM prediction falls short of that same offer by 16 points, so improving one unit from Merit to Distinction becomes the difference that closes the gap.

BTEC vs A-Level Points at the Same Tariff Level

D*D*D* at Extended Diploma level matches A*A*A* at A-level, both worth 168 points. DDM roughly matches BBC at A-level in points terms. These are headline comparisons rather than an exhaustive cross-table, since the two qualifications measure different skills even when their tariff points align.

Want to move up a grade band before results day? Checking your points is often the first step toward realising you need to improve a specific unit grade, not just confirm what you already have.

How many UCAS points is a BTEC Distinction worth?

It depends on qualification size. A single Distinction at Extended Certificate size is worth 48 points, but within an Extended Diploma, one D contributes toward a combination such as DDD, worth 144 points overall, rather than 48 points on its own.

Is BTEC Extended Diploma equivalent to 3 A-levels for UCAS points?

Yes. D*D*D* carries 168 points, identical to three A* grades at A-level under the current UCAS Tariff.

Do all universities accept BTEC UCAS points?

Most do for many courses, though some competitive or science-heavy courses set entry requirements around specific A-level subjects rather than a pure points total. Always check the individual course's entry requirements directly.

Where can I check my exact BTEC UCAS points?

Use Pearson's or UCAS's official tariff calculator with your specific qualification size and predicted or achieved grade combination for a confirmed figure.
