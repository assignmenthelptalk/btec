This page covers the Pearson BTEC Level 5 Higher National Diploma (HND). It sets out the structure, the two Pearson-set units, how the overall grade is calculated, and what Pearson says about resubmission and progression. It follows Pearson's Higher Nationals in Business specification (Issue 3, January 2022, first teaching September 2021), the Higher National suite we can check against a published specification. Other subjects have their own specifications, so check the one for your programme. For the Level 4 Certificate, see our HNC page.

What the BTEC HND Is

The HND is a Level 5 qualification of 240 credits: 120 at Level 4 (eight units) and 120 at Level 5 (seven units). Pearson says it is usually studied full time over two years, or part time over four. It is part of the Higher Nationals at Levels 4 and 5, which Pearson says follow the Quality Assurance Agency's Framework for Higher Education qualification standards. Pearson also lists Higher Nationals at Levels 6 and 7 in its wider range, so the HND is not the top of the Higher National suite.

Pearson says the Level 5 units let students specialise in a business-related area and prepare them to move on to specific areas of business at Level 6 or to enter employment in roles that need personal responsibility and decision-making. Pearson describes the aim as students who can deal with uncertainty and complexity, demonstrate critical evaluation, and use both theory and practice in a wide range of business situations.

HND Pathways and Level 5 Units

Specialist pathways appear in brackets in the qualification title. Pearson lists these for the Business HND: Accounting and Finance, Entrepreneurship and Small Business Management, Human Resource Management, Management, Marketing, Procurement and Supply Management and Law. A student on the general Business route has no bracketed pathway. You can only be assessed for the qualification you are registered on, so if your centre moves you to another pathway your registration must be transferred.

The Two Pearson-Set Units

Pearson says the HND has two compulsory Pearson-set units: one at Level 4 (Unit 6 Managing a Successful Business Project) and one at Level 5 (Unit 19 Research Project). Pearson decides a theme each year. The centre sets the assignment, the centre grades it, and there is no Pearson-set exam.

  • Unit 6: Managing a Successful Business Project (Level 4, 15 credits). The learning outcomes are to explain the key stages of the project lifecycle, produce a Project Management Plan using primary and secondary research methods, implement the plan to communicate results and make conclusions from the evidence, and reflect on the value gained from the project and the project management process.
  • Unit 19: Research Project (Level 5). Students choose their own project based on the Pearson theme and, unless they are on the general business pathway, it must relate to their specialist pathway. The learning outcomes are to examine appropriate research methodologies and methods, develop a research proposal with a supporting literature review, analyse data using appropriate techniques to communicate findings, and reflect on the application of research methodologies and the process. The content includes the distinct phases of research and the difference between positivist (deductive) and interpretivist (inductive) paradigms.

Pearson's specification does not set a word count for either unit. Your centre sets the length and the referencing style in the Assignment Brief, so follow the brief. Pearson says centres should give students a guide on how to use and reference source materials and avoid plagiarism. See referencing for BTEC HNC and HND.

How the HND Is Graded

Each unit is graded Pass, Merit or Distinction against its grading grid, and Pearson's points table gives 4, 6 and 8 points per credit. The overall grade uses the same boundaries as the HNC: Pass 420 to 599, Merit 600 to 839 and Distinction 840 and above. One difference matters. For the HND, the overall qualification grade is based on performance in the Level 5 units only. So the Level 4 units must be passed, but they do not set your overall HND grade.

To be awarded the HND, a student must complete units worth 120 credits at Level 5 and at least a Pass in 105 of them, and complete 120 credits at Level 4 with at least a Pass in 105 of them. A student who attempted but did not achieve a Pass in one 15-credit unit at Level 4 and one at Level 5 can still be awarded the HND if they completed and passed the remaining units at both levels. See BTEC grading criteria explained.

What separates Merit from Distinction in an HND assignment? The answer is in the criteria your brief targets. Read each command verb, such as analyse, evaluate or critically analyse, and check that every part of your answer does what that verb asks for.

If an HND Assignment Is Not Passed

Pearson says a student whose assignment does not pass must have the opportunity to resubmit it, once only, and the grade for a reassessed assignment is capped at a Pass. A student who still fails the unit may be allowed to repeat it once, again capped at a Pass. Ask your centre how it schedules resubmissions. See our resubmission guide.

Progression from the HND

Pearson says that once students have achieved the HND they can enter or continue employment, link with the appropriate professional body, commit to continuing professional development, or go to university. It says higher education providers recognise the Level 5 HND as meeting admission requirements to many relevant business-related courses, and it gives examples such as BA (Hons) Business Management and BSc (Hons) Accounting. Pearson says many universities allow advanced entry to the second or third year, through credit transfer, articulation agreements or case-by-case admission, and that each university sets its own admission criteria, which can change. Check the course entry requirements for the subject and year you want, and see Pearson's degree finder at highernationals.com.

Pearson adds that the Law pathway is based on English law and does not by itself qualify someone for a legal profession: a law degree is often needed, and a 2.1 is usually required to become a barrister or solicitor, followed by professional training. Students on that pathway should check articulation agreements with their centre.

What is the Managing a Successful Business Project unit at BTEC HND?

In Pearson's Higher Nationals in Business it is Unit 6, a Level 4 core unit of 15 credits, and it is Pearson-set. Pearson provides an annual theme, the centre sets the project brief and the centre grades it. HND students also complete the Level 5 Research Project (Unit 19). The specification does not set a word count, so follow your Assignment Brief.

What does the Level 5 Research Project involve?

Unit 19 asks you to examine research methodologies and methods, develop a research proposal with a supporting literature review, analyse data using appropriate techniques, and reflect on the research process. You choose a project within Pearson's annual theme, related to your specialist pathway unless you are on the general business pathway. It is graded by your centre, and the specification gives no word count.

What grades do I need on my BTEC HND to go on to a degree?

Pearson does not set a grade, because each university sets its own admission criteria and they can change. Pearson says the HND is recognised as meeting admission requirements to many related business courses, and that many universities allow advanced entry to the second or third year. Check the entry requirements for the course and year you want, and ask your centre about any articulation agreement.

Does the HND grade include my Level 4 units?

Not for the overall grade. Pearson says that for the HND, the overall qualification grade is based on your performance in the Level 5 units only. You must still complete the Level 4 units and achieve at least a Pass in 105 of those 120 credits.
